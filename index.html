About

Practice

Advocate Azhar Baba practices in Srinagar, Jammu & Kashmir. He represents clients across District Courts in Srinagar and the High Court of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. With years of courtroom experience, he handles criminal law, civil litigation, family disputes, property matters, bail applications, consumer cases, motor accident claims, and constitutional matters.

Commitment

Committed to upholding the rights of individuals and ensuring access to justice for the people of Kashmir. Chamber located in Srinagar city.
